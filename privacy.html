Privacy

Your images are processed entirely inside your browser. They are never uploaded, stored, or seen by anyone else.

What happens to the pictures you add

When you drop in a picture or pick one from your device, the browser decodes it locally, draws it onto the 3D model, and renders the result on your own machine. Downloading the PNG writes a file straight from the browser to your device.

At no point is the image sent over the network. There is no upload endpoint, no image storage, and no processing server — closing the tab discards everything.

What the site loads

Only its own files: the page itself, the 3D models, and the placeholder images. There are no third-party scripts, no external fonts, and no embedded trackers.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no browser storage.

Analytics

Currently none. If you add analytics, say which tool, what it records, and whether it's configured to run without consent (CNIL exemption) — otherwise you also need a consent banner, and this section has to change.

Server logs

Your host almost certainly logs IP addresses and user agents for every request. Name the host, say what it retains and for how long. This is the part most "we collect nothing" policies get wrong.

Your rights

Under the GDPR: access, rectification, erasure, objection, and the right to complain to the CNIL. Since no personal data is collected through the tool itself, this mostly concerns server logs and anything sent to you by email. Give the contact address for such requests.

Contact

Email address for privacy questions, and the date this policy was last updated.
