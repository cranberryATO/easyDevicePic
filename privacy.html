Privacy

Your images are processed entirely inside your browser. They are never uploaded, stored, or seen by anyone else. This page is a courtesy translation — the French politique de confidentialité is the authoritative version.

Data controller

André Tousch, publisher of this site (see the legal notice). For anything concerning your data: andre@andretousch.com.

What happens to the pictures you add

When you drop in a picture or pick one from your device, the browser decodes it locally, draws it onto the 3D model, and renders the result on your own machine. Downloading the PNG writes a file straight from the browser to your device.

At no point is the image sent over the network. There is no upload endpoint, no image storage, and no processing server — closing the tab discards everything.

What the site loads

Only its own files: the page itself, the 3D models, and the placeholder images. There are no third-party scripts, no external CDN, no remote fonts — the text uses the fonts already installed on your system — and no embedded trackers.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. Your language choice is kept in your browser's local storage, on your device only: it serves solely to show you the site in the language you picked, allows no tracking, and is never read by a server. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data. No consent banner is required for this use alone.

Server logs

The server automatically records, for each request: the IP address, the date and time, the file requested, the response code and the volume transferred, the browser type (user agent) and, where applicable, the page the link was followed from.

These logs are used to keep the site secure, to detect abuse, to measure traffic and to diagnose failures. The legal basis is the publisher's legitimate interest in the security and proper operation of the service (GDPR article 6(1)(f)). They are kept for six months at most — log rotation on the server deletes them automatically past that point — they are disclosed to no one, save a judicial request, and are used neither for profiling nor for advertising.

If you email us

When you send an email, your address and the content of your message are kept for as long as it takes to handle your request and, where relevant, the relationship that follows — at most three years after our last exchange. The legal basis is pre-contractual steps taken at your request, or the legitimate interest in answering messages received.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data, as well as to set directives on what becomes of it after your death. These rights are exercised by email to andre@andretousch.com; you will receive a reply within one month.

Since the tool itself collects nothing, these rights concern in practice the server logs and email correspondence. As an IP address alone does not let the publisher identify you, further information may be requested in order to locate the records that relate to you (GDPR article 11).

You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority — 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07 — or at www.cnil.fr.
