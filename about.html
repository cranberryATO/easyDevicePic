About

easyDevicePic puts a screenshot on a 3D phone or laptop, lets you turn it to any angle, and gives you back a PNG with a transparent background.

It's free, needs no account, and runs entirely in your browser — your screenshots never leave your machine. See the privacy page for what that means in practice.
